## 1. Our commitment

{{legal_name}}, trading as {{trading_name}} ("BukkaPay"), is committed to preventing its Services from being used for money laundering, terrorist financing, proliferation financing, sanctions evasion, fraud or other financial crime. We comply with the anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing laws of every country where we operate — for example the Financial Intelligence Centre Act in South Africa — and work with our licensed Partner Institutions, which have their own obligations under those laws.

This statement summarises our programme. It does not describe every control, as doing so would help criminals avoid them.

## 2. Governance

Our board is responsible for the programme. A designated compliance officer oversees day-to-day compliance, reports regularly to senior management and is the point of contact for regulators and financial intelligence units. Our programme is reviewed at least annually and is independently tested.

## 3. Risk-based approach

We assess the money laundering and terrorist financing risks of our customers, products, countries, delivery channels and payment corridors, and apply stronger controls where risk is higher. Some countries, corridors, business types and transactions are not supported because the risk is too high — see our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 4. Customer due diligence

- **Identity verification:** we verify every customer's identity before they can use services that move money beyond low limits, using document checks, liveness checks and trusted data sources.
- **Tiered limits:** account limits depend on how much verification has been completed and on the customer's country.
- **Businesses:** we verify the business, its directors and its beneficial owners, and understand the nature of the business before it can accept payments at higher limits. Informal sellers, such as street vendors, may start with their verified personal ID within individual seller limits.
- **Enhanced due diligence:** we apply additional checks for higher-risk customers, including politically exposed persons, and may ask about the source of funds or wealth.
- **Ongoing due diligence:** we keep customer information up to date and re-verify where needed.

## 5. Sanctions and screening

We screen customers, beneficial owners and, where relevant, counterparties against applicable sanctions lists (including those of the United Nations and of the countries where we operate) and politically exposed person lists, at onboarding and on an ongoing basis. We do not provide services to sanctioned persons or for prohibited purposes.

## 6. Transaction monitoring

We monitor transactions for unusual patterns, such as activity that does not fit a customer's profile, rapid movement of funds, structuring to avoid limits, or links to known fraud. Alerts are reviewed by trained compliance staff.

## 7. Reporting

Where we know or suspect that funds are connected to crime, we report it to the relevant financial intelligence unit as the law requires. By law we may not tell the customer that a report has been made. We may delay, block or reverse transactions, or restrict or close accounts, where the law requires or where we suspect financial crime.

## 8. Record keeping

We keep customer due diligence and transaction records for at least five years after a business relationship ends or a transaction completes, or longer where the law requires.

## 9. Training

All staff receive anti-money laundering training when they join and at least annually, appropriate to their role.

## 10. Your part

We may ask you for information or documents to meet our obligations. If you do not provide them, we may have to limit or close your account. Giving false information is an offence.
