## 5. Records we hold

We hold records in the following categories:

- **Company records:** incorporation documents, statutory registers, minutes and resolutions.
- **Financial records:** financial statements, accounting records, tax records and banking records.
- **Customer and merchant records:** account information, identity verification records, transaction records, correspondence and complaints.
- **Employee records:** employment contracts, payroll, benefits, performance and disciplinary records.
- **Supplier and partner records:** agreements with Partner Institutions, payment providers, technology providers and other suppliers.
- **Compliance records:** anti-money laundering records, regulatory correspondence, policies and incident registers.
- **Technology records:** system documentation, security logs and audit trails.

Records held under other legislation include those required by the Companies Act, the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, the Income Tax Act, the Value-Added Tax Act, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act, the Labour Relations Act, the Consumer Protection Act, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act and POPIA.

## 6. Processing of personal information

**Purpose of processing:** to provide payment and wallet services, verify identity, prevent financial crime, meet legal and regulatory obligations, support customers, employ staff and manage suppliers — as described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

**Categories of data subjects and information:**

- **Customers and merchants:** identity and contact details, identity documents and biometric verification data, financial and transaction information, device and security information.
- **Business owners and directors:** identity and contact details, ownership information.
- **Employees and applicants:** identity and contact details, qualifications, employment and payroll information.
- **Suppliers and partners:** company and contact details, banking details.

**Recipients:** Partner Institutions, payment providers and card issuers, identity verification and screening providers, technology hosting providers, professional advisers, regulators and law enforcement where the law requires.

**Cross-border transfers:** some of our technology providers store information outside South Africa, including in the European Union and the United States. Transfers are made only in accordance with section 72 of POPIA.

**Security measures:** encryption in transit and of sensitive data at rest, access controls and multi-factor authentication, audit logging, row-level database security, backups, staff training and incident response procedures, as described in our [Data Breach Response and Notification Policy](/legal/data-breach).

## 7. How to request access

1. Complete **Form 2** (Request for Access to Record) prescribed under PAIA, available from the Information Regulator's website or from our Information Officer.
2. Send it to the Information Officer at {{information_officer_email}} or to the address above.
3. Provide proof of your identity, and if you are acting for someone else, proof of your authority.
4. Pay the prescribed request fee where it applies (requests for your own personal information are free). An access fee may be payable for reproducing records, as prescribed.

We will respond within 30 days, which may be extended once by up to 30 days in the circumstances PAIA allows.

## 8. Grounds for refusal

We may refuse access on the grounds set out in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA, including to protect the privacy of third parties, commercial information of third parties or of BukkaPay, confidential information, the safety of individuals and property, records privileged from production in legal proceedings, and where disclosure would prejudice the prevention or detection of crime.

## 9. Requests about your personal information (POPIA)

You may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you, to correct or delete it (using Form 2 under the POPIA Regulations), or object to its processing (using Form 1), by contacting the Information Officer. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you are not satisfied with our response.
