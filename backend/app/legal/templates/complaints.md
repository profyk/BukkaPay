## 1. Our commitment

We want to put things right when they go wrong. This policy explains how to complain to {{trading_name}} ({{legal_name}}), how we will handle your complaint, and where you can go if you are not satisfied. Making a complaint is free.

## 2. How to complain

- **In the app:** open Help and start a chat with support.
- **By email:** {{complaints_email}}
- **By phone:** {{phone}}

Please tell us your name, the email or phone number on your account, what went wrong, the date and reference of any payment involved, and what you would like us to do. Never send us your PIN, password or one-time codes.

If your complaint is about a payment you did not authorise, or you think your account has been compromised, tell us immediately so we can protect your account.

## 3. What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement:** we acknowledge your complaint within **2 business days**, give you a reference number and tell you who is handling it.
2. **Investigation:** we look at the facts fairly, including information from you, our records and, where relevant, our Partner Institutions, payment providers or the merchant.
3. **Response:** we aim to resolve complaints within **15 business days**. If it will take longer, for example because we need information from a bank or card network, we will tell you why and when to expect our answer. Our final response will explain our decision and any redress we are offering.

Card disputes follow Visa's rules and timelines, which can take longer — see our [Refunds, Reversals and Chargebacks Policy](/legal/refunds).

## 4. If you are not satisfied

If you are unhappy with our final response, or we have not responded within the time required, you may refer your complaint, free of charge, to the independent ombud or regulator for your country. For example, in South Africa:

- payment and financial services complaints: the National Financial Ombud Scheme South Africa;
- personal information complaints: the Information Regulator.

Complaints about a Partner Institution's licensed services may also be referred under that institution's complaints process. We will tell you which body applies to your complaint in our final response.

## 5. Complaints about merchants

If you paid a merchant and have a problem with the goods or services, please contact the merchant first. If that does not resolve it, contact us and we will help where we can, including through card dispute rights where they apply.

## 6. Records and improvement

We keep a record of every complaint and our response for at least five years, and we review complaint trends regularly to improve our Services.
