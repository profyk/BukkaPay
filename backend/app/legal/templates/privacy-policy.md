## 2. What we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Identity and contact details:** name, date of birth, ID or passport number, nationality, phone number, email address and home address.
- **Verification information:** photos of your identity document, a selfie or liveness check, proof of address and, for businesses, company registration documents, directors' and owners' details.
- **Financial information:** your balances, payments, payment links, invoices, contributions, withdrawals, beneficiaries and verified bank account details.
- **Communications:** messages you send us, support chats, complaints and survey answers.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Device and usage information:** device model, operating system, app version, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, log-in times, and how you use the app — used for security and to keep the Services working.
- **Security information:** passkey and session identifiers. Your transaction PIN is stored only in a one-way hashed form; we cannot see it.

**Information from others**

- Identity verification, sanctions and politically-exposed-person screening providers.
- Payment providers, banks and our card issuer when you add money, pay, receive or withdraw.
- Google or Apple, if you choose to sign in with them: your name, email address and an account identifier. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
- Merchants and other users, for example when someone sends you a payment request.

We do not collect card numbers or CVVs on our own servers: card details are handled by our licensed payment providers and card issuer.

## 3. Google user data and Sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", BukkaPay asks Google only for the basic sign-in permissions — openid, email and profile. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

- **What we access:** your name, email address, whether Google has verified that email address, your profile picture (if you have one) and a Google account identifier.
- **How we use it:** only to create your BukkaPay account or sign you in to it, to pre-fill your name and email, and to keep your account secure. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to build or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
- **How we store it:** with your other account information in our access-controlled, encrypted database. We never receive or store your Google password.
- **Who we share it with:** we do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties, except with the technology providers that host our systems (listed in section 6) to run the Services, or where the law requires it.
- **How to remove it:** you can disconnect BukkaPay at any time from your Google Account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and ask us to delete your account information by emailing {{privacy_email}}. Records we must keep by law are retained as described in section 8.

BukkaPay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Sign in with Apple works the same way: we receive only your name, email address (or an Apple private relay address) and an account identifier, and use them only to create and sign in to your account.

## 4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your information to:

- **provide the Services** — open and run your account, process payments, show balances and history (to perform our contract with you);
- **verify your identity and prevent financial crime** — know-your-customer checks, sanctions screening, fraud and money-laundering monitoring (to comply with the law, and our legitimate interest in protecting you, us and the financial system);
- **keep the Services secure** — detect suspicious log-ins, protect accounts, investigate incidents (legitimate interests and legal obligation);
- **support you and handle complaints** (contract and legal obligation);
- **meet legal and regulatory duties** — record-keeping, reporting to regulators and authorities, responding to lawful requests (legal obligation);
- **improve the Services** — understand how features are used, fix problems (legitimate interests; we use aggregated or de-identified information where we can);
- **tell you about products and offers** — only where you have agreed or the law otherwise allows; you can opt out at any time (consent or legitimate interests).

We do not sell your personal information.

- device and security logs are kept for up to 24 months unless needed for an investigation.

When we no longer need information we delete it or de-identify it.
