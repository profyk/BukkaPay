## 2. What we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Identity and contact details:** name, username, date of birth, ID or passport number, nationality, country of residence, phone number, email address and home address.
- **Verification information:** photos of your identity document, a selfie or liveness check, proof of address and, for businesses, company registration documents, tax numbers, and directors' and owners' details.
- **Financial information:** your wallets and balances, payments, payment links, invoices, trade escrows, contributions, cash deposits and withdrawals at agents, transfers abroad and the recipients you save (name, bank or mobile-money account), withdrawals and verified bank account details.
- **Business information** (merchants and cash agents): business name and type, address and opening hours, staff names and roles, settlement details, and the location you choose to show customers.
- **Communications:** messages you send us, support chats, complaints, survey answers and the reason you give if you close your account.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Device and usage information:** device model, operating system, app version, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, log-in times, and how you use the app — used for security, fraud prevention and to keep the Services working.
- **Security information:** passkey and session identifiers and push-notification tokens. Your password and transaction PIN are stored only in a one-way hashed form; we cannot see them.
- **We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking** in our apps, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

**App permissions** (each is optional and asked for only when you use the feature)

- **Camera:** to scan QR codes and, during verification, to take photos of your ID document and a selfie.
- **Photos:** to upload an ID document or a profile picture you choose.
- **Face ID / fingerprint:** to unlock the app. Biometric checks happen on your device; we never receive your fingerprint or face data.
- **Notifications:** to send payment alerts and security messages. You can turn categories off in Notification settings.
- **Location** (merchant dashboard only): if a cash agent chooses to pin their shop on the map. The customer app does not access your location; you find agents by typing a town.

**Information from others**

- Identity verification, sanctions and politically-exposed-person screening providers.
- Payment providers, banks, mobile-money operators, payout partners and our card issuer when you add money, pay, receive, send abroad or withdraw.
- Google or Apple, if you choose to sign in with them (see section 3).
- Merchants, cash agents and other users — for example when someone pays you, requests money from you, or serves you at a cash agent.
- Public sources and fraud-prevention databases, where the law allows, to verify businesses and prevent financial crime.

We do not collect full card numbers or CVVs on our own servers: card details are handled by our licensed card issuer and payment providers, who are certified under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

## 3. Google user data and Sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", BukkaPay asks Google only for the basic sign-in permissions — openid, email and profile. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

- **What we access:** your name, email address, whether Google has verified that email address, your profile picture (if you have one) and a Google account identifier.
- **How we use it:** only to create your BukkaPay account or sign you in to it, to pre-fill your name and email, and to keep your account secure. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to build or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
- **What we ask you for in addition:** when you create an account with Google we also ask you to choose a username, enter your phone number and choose your country of residence. This information comes from you, not from Google.
- **How we store it:** with your other account information in our access-controlled database, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, hosted by our cloud providers. Only staff who need it to support your account can see it, and staff actions on customer accounts are recorded in an audit log. We never receive or store your Google password, and we do not keep Google access or refresh tokens: we check Google's signed ID token once, when you sign in, and then discard it.
- **How long we keep it:** for as long as your BukkaPay account is open. When you delete your account, your Google-derived profile picture and sign-in link are deleted immediately; your name and email address are kept only inside the sealed identity record the law requires (see section 9) and then deleted.
- **Who we share it with:** we do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties, except with the technology providers that host our systems (listed in section 7) to run the Services, or where the law requires it.
- **How to remove it:** you can disconnect BukkaPay at any time from your Google Account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and delete your BukkaPay account as described in section 11.

BukkaPay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Sign in with Apple works the same way: we receive only your name, email address (or an Apple private relay address) and an account identifier, and use them only to create and sign in to your account.

## 4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your information to:

- **provide the Services** — open and run your account, process payments, transfers, cash deposits and withdrawals, show balances, history and spending insights (to perform our contract with you);
- **verify your identity and prevent financial crime** — know-your-customer checks, sanctions and politically-exposed-person screening, fraud and money-laundering monitoring, and reporting to authorities (to comply with the law, and our legitimate interest in protecting you, us and the financial system);
- **keep the Services and your account secure** — detect suspicious log-ins, protect accounts, enforce limits, investigate incidents (legitimate interests and legal obligation);
- **charge and explain fees** — work out fees, the monthly account fee and any waivers you qualify for (contract);
- **support you and handle complaints and disputes** (contract and legal obligation);
- **meet legal, tax and regulatory duties** — record-keeping, safeguarding customer money, reporting to regulators and authorities, responding to lawful requests (legal obligation);
- **improve the Services** — understand how features are used and fix problems, using aggregated or de-identified information where we can (legitimate interests);
- **tell you about products and offers** — only where you have agreed or the law otherwise allows; you can opt out at any time (consent or legitimate interests).

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced them against your rights and you can object (section 11).

## 6. Compliance, anti-money laundering and your information

BukkaPay is a regulated financial service. To meet anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing and sanctions laws in the countries we operate in (for example South Africa's Financial Intelligence Centre Act, Nigeria's Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act and Kenya's Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act) we must:

- verify the identity of customers, business owners and cash agents before and while they use the Services, and keep those records;
- screen customers, businesses and payment recipients against sanctions and politically-exposed-person lists, at onboarding and regularly after that;
- monitor transactions for unusual activity and apply limits that depend on how far you are verified;
- report suspicious transactions and certain large transactions to the authorities. **The law can prohibit us from telling you that we have made such a report**, and in that case we cannot answer requests about it;
- keep identity and transaction records for the periods the law sets, even after you close your account (section 9);
- hold customer money with licensed Partner Institutions in safeguarded accounts, separate from BukkaPay's own money.

These obligations are why some of your information cannot be deleted on request until the legal period ends, and why we may need more information from you before you can use some features.

## 9. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet legal requirements, then delete it or de-identify it so it can no longer identify you. Our retention schedule:

- **Account and profile** (name, username, contact details, photo, preferences): while your account is open. Deleted from the live account immediately when you delete it.
- **Identity verification records** (ID document details, verification results, business ownership): while your account is open and for **5 years after it closes** — longer where your country's law requires. On closure they are moved into a sealed record that only our compliance team can access, and deleted automatically when the period ends.
- **Transaction and ledger records** (payments, transfers, fees, cash deposits and withdrawals): **5 years after the transaction** (or longer where tax or financial law requires). These records are anonymised against your closed account and cannot be edited.
- **Sanctions screening and transaction-monitoring records, and reports to authorities:** at least 5 years, as the law requires.
- **Support chats and complaints:** up to 5 years after the case closes.
- **Device, session and security logs:** up to 24 months, unless needed for an investigation.
- **Staff audit logs** (who did what on customer accounts): 5 years.
- **Push-notification tokens and device records:** until you sign out, turn notifications off or delete your account.
- **Marketing preferences:** until you change them or delete your account.
- **Backups:** our providers keep encrypted backups for a short rolling period (up to 30 days), after which deleted data disappears from them too.

## 10. How we protect it

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption of sensitive data at rest; private, access-restricted storage for identity documents;
- one-way hashing of passwords and transaction PINs, transaction PINs on every payment, biometric and passkey sign-in, and the ability to sign out other devices;
- least-privilege access for staff, multi-factor authentication for administrators, row-level security on our database, and an audit log of every privileged action;
- "maker-checker" controls: high-risk actions such as manual balance corrections need two different people, and no one — including administrators — can type in or edit a balance;
- a double-entry ledger, daily reconciliation and fraud monitoring.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires — see our [Data Breach Response and Notification Policy](/legal/data-breach).

**Deleting your account**

You can delete your account at any time:

1. **In the app:** Profile → Delete account. Withdraw any remaining balance first (a closed account can't hold money) and cancel any unused cash withdrawal code. Type DELETE and enter your PIN.
2. **Without the app:** follow the steps at [bukkapay.com/delete-account](/delete-account), or email {{privacy_email}} from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We verify it's you within 2 business days and close the account within 30 days.

When your account is deleted:

- it closes immediately and you are signed out on every device;
- your profile, photo, username, phone number, password, PIN, biometric setting, Apple and Google sign-in links, saved contacts and beneficiaries, devices and notification settings are deleted;
- identity verification and transaction records are kept in a sealed record for the legal period in section 9, then deleted automatically;
- we email you a confirmation.

You can also disconnect Google from BukkaPay at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and Apple at Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple on your Apple device.
