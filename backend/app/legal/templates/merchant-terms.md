## 3. Onboarding and verification

You must give us true and complete information about yourself and your business and keep it up to date.

We may ask for further information at any time, including about your products, customers, website, licences and source of funds. We may decline to onboard a merchant, or limit the services available, at our discretion and as the law requires.

You are responsible for the actions of your staff and anyone using your account or API keys.

## 5. When a payment counts as received

A payment link, invoice or QR payment is marked paid only when our systems confirm the money has arrived. Do not release goods on the strength of a screenshot, SMS or email that claims payment has been made.

## 6. Fees

Our merchant fees are shown in the merchant dashboard and on our pricing page and may differ by country, payment method and plan. We deduct fees from the payments you receive unless agreed otherwise. We will give you at least 30 days' notice of fee increases.

## 7. Payouts

You can request payouts to a verified bank account in your name or your business's name. A payout shows as settled only once the receiving bank confirms it. Payout timing may depend on the bank, the payment rail and our risk checks.

## 8. Refunds, reversals and chargebacks

You can refund a customer from the dashboard. If a payment is reversed, disputed or charged back, the amount — and any related fee charged by the payment provider or card network — may be deducted from your balance or future payments. You must provide evidence to help us respond to disputes within the time we ask. See our [Refunds, Reversals and Chargebacks Policy](/legal/refunds).

## 9. Reserves, holds and set-off

To manage risk, for example where we see high refund or chargeback rates, signs of fraud, or a change in your business, we may:

- delay payouts or hold part of your balance as a reserve for a reasonable period;
- set off amounts you owe us against your balance or future payments;
- reduce your limits.

We will tell you when we do this unless the law prohibits it.
