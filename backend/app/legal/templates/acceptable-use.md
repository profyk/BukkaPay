## 2. Prohibited activities

You must not use BukkaPay for, or in connection with:

- anything illegal in your country, the recipient's country or the country where the Services are provided;
- money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion or receiving the proceeds of crime;
- fraud, scams, phishing, impersonation, or obtaining money under false pretences;
- pyramid or Ponzi schemes, "money doubling", investment clubs promising guaranteed returns, or other deceptive schemes;
- using someone else's identity or account, or letting someone else use yours ("money muling");
- splitting payments to avoid limits or verification;
- harassment, abuse, threats or extortion, including in payment references.

## 3. Prohibited businesses

Merchants may not accept payments for:

- illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, or prescription medicines sold without the required licence;
- weapons, ammunition, explosives and their components, unless licensed and approved by us in advance;
- unlicensed gambling, lotteries or betting;
- adult content or services, and any content involving the exploitation of minors or non-consensual content;
- counterfeit, stolen or pirated goods, or goods that infringe intellectual property;
- unlicensed financial services, including money transmission, foreign exchange, lending, deposit-taking or investment services;
- cryptocurrency exchange or trading services, unless licensed and approved by us in advance;
- wildlife products, human remains or organs, or goods prohibited by the law of the buyer's or seller's country;
- any business that we or our partners consider too high-risk, as notified to you.

Some businesses need our written approval before using merchant services, and may need to show us licences, for example: alcohol and tobacco, pharmacies, travel agents, charities and fundraising, dating services, telemarketing, subscription services, and high-value goods such as jewellery and electronics.

You must not:
