## 1. About this policy

This policy explains when payments made with {{trading_name}} can be refunded, reversed or disputed. It forms part of our [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms), [Merchant Terms](/legal/merchant-terms) and [Virtual Visa Card Terms](/legal/cardholder-terms).

## 2. Payments you made by mistake

Wallet-to-wallet payments, payment-link payments and bill payments are generally final once you confirm them. If you paid the wrong person or the wrong amount, contact us straight away at {{support_email}}. We will contact the recipient and try to recover the money, but we cannot take money back from another customer without their agreement or a legal basis to do so.

## 3. Payments you did not authorise

If a payment was made from your account without your authorisation, tell us as soon as you notice it, and in any event within **13 months** of the payment date (or any other period set by the law that applies to you). Where the law entitles you to a refund we will refund the payment, normally by the end of the next business day after you tell us, unless we have reasonable grounds to suspect fraud on your part, in which case we will investigate first.

You may not be entitled to a refund if you acted fraudulently, or if you intentionally or with gross negligence failed to keep your PIN, password, one-time codes or device secure.

## 4. Payments we executed incorrectly

If a payment was not made, was made late, or was made for the wrong amount because of our error, we will correct it and refund any fees you were charged for it.

## 5. Cross-border payments

If a cross-border payment cannot be delivered, the full amount, including our fee, is returned to your wallet. Once a cross-border payment has been delivered to the recipient, it cannot be reversed by us.

## 6. Refunds from merchants

If you want a refund for goods or services, ask the merchant. Merchants set their own refund policies, subject to consumer protection law. When a merchant issues a refund through BukkaPay, it is returned to the wallet or card you paid with. Fees paid on the original payment may not be refundable unless the law requires it.

## 7. Card disputes and chargebacks

For payments made with your BukkaPay virtual Visa card, you may be able to dispute a transaction under Visa's rules — for example if you were charged twice, the goods were not delivered, or the payment was not authorised. Raise a dispute in the app or at {{support_email}} as soon as possible, and normally within **120 days** of the transaction. We may ask you for evidence. While a dispute is investigated, we may give you a temporary credit, which will be reversed if the dispute is decided in the merchant's favour.

## 8. Top-up reversals

If a top-up to your wallet is reversed or charged back by your bank or card issuer, we may deduct the amount from your balance. If this leaves your balance negative, you must repay it.

## 9. For merchants

If a customer's payment to you is refunded, reversed or charged back, the amount and any associated network or provider fee may be deducted from your balance or future payouts, as described in the [Merchant Terms](/legal/merchant-terms). Respond to dispute requests with evidence within the time we ask, or the dispute may be decided against you.
