## 2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Local storage and similar technologies work in a comparable way. They let a site remember things — for example that you are signed in.

## 3. The cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** These are needed for the site and apps to work and cannot be switched off. They keep you signed in, protect against fraud and cross-site request forgery, balance traffic, and remember security choices. Examples: session and authentication tokens, security tokens.

**Preferences.** These remember choices such as your language, country or dismissed notices. They are stored on your device only.

**Analytics.** We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce analytics, we will update this policy and, where the law requires, ask for your consent first.

We do not use cookies to show you advertising or to sell information about you.

## 4. Third-party content

Some pages may load content from other services, such as app store badges or the Google and Apple sign-in buttons. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies when you interact with them.

## 5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the site — such as signing in — will not work.
