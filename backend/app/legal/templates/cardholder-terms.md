## 1. About these terms

These Virtual Visa Card Terms apply to the virtual Visa card linked to your BukkaPay wallet (the "Card"). They are in addition to our [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms).

The Card is issued by {{card_issuer}} (the "Issuer") pursuant to a licence from Visa, and is programme-managed by {{legal_name}}, trading as {{trading_name}}. The Issuer's own cardholder terms also apply and are shown to you when the Card is created.

## 2. Getting a Card

Each wallet can have a virtual Card once you have completed the required identity verification and Cards are available in your country. The Card is a **prepaid / debit card**: it spends from your wallet balance in the Card's currency. It is not a credit card and you cannot spend more than your available balance.

## 3. Using your Card

You can use the Card to pay online and, once added to Apple Pay or Google Pay, to tap to pay in stores, wherever Visa is accepted, subject to the limits shown in the app.

Card details are shown securely in the app. BukkaPay does not store your full card number or CVV on its own servers — they are held by the Issuer and its processor.

A Card payment is authorised when you, or someone using your Card details or device, completes the transaction. Some merchants, such as hotels and car hire companies, may place a temporary hold on your balance.

## 4. Fees and foreign currency

Card fees, including any maintenance or foreign transaction fees, are shown in the app before they apply. If you pay in a currency other than the Card's currency, the amount is converted at the rate set by Visa or the Issuer on the day the transaction is processed, plus any fee shown in the app.

## 5. Keeping your Card safe

- Do not share your Card details, PIN, one-time codes or device with anyone.
- Freeze your Card in the app immediately if you think it has been compromised. You can unfreeze it if you find it was a false alarm.
- Tell us immediately if you see a transaction you do not recognise.

## 6. Disputes and chargebacks

If there is a problem with a Card payment — for example goods were not delivered, you were charged twice, or you did not authorise it — contact the merchant first if appropriate, then raise a dispute with us in the app or at {{support_email}} as soon as possible and within the time limits in our [Refunds, Reversals and Chargebacks Policy](/legal/refunds). We will raise a chargeback under Visa's rules where you are eligible. The outcome depends on those rules and the evidence available.

## 7. Blocking and cancelling the Card

We or the Issuer may block, suspend or cancel the Card if we suspect fraud or unauthorised use, if your account is restricted, if required by law, Visa or the Issuer, or if these terms are breached. You may cancel the Card in the app at any time. Cancelling the Card does not close your wallet.

## 8. Expiry

The Card expires on the date shown in the app. We may issue a replacement before it expires unless your account is closed or restricted.
